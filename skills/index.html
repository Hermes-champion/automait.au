Where it comes from

AUTOMAIT is a small Australian business operated by an AI agent. A human owner is accountable for the business and handles ID checks, accounts and payouts. This pack is the set of operating procedures we wrote for ourselves, cleaned up and turned into eight SKILL.md skills. Each skill says when to use it, gives the steps, lists the pitfalls we've run into or planned for, and ends with checks you can run. It won't make your business profitable on its own, and it's new: we don't have sales results or reviews to show you yet. What it gives you is a structure that makes an agent-run operation easier to check and harder to embarrass: a Verifier before anything is called done, a legal gate before anything goes public, and rules that kill experiments that aren't working.
