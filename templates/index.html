Questions

Something else? Email hello@automait.au.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Use File → Import → Upload. In the quote kit, the GST switch and totals work there.

Are quote prices entered with or without GST?

Without GST. If you're registered, the sheet adds 10% to lines marked Y.

Does the tracker tell me how much tax I'll pay?

No. You choose a set-aside % (for example with your accountant), and the sheet tracks what you've put aside against that.

Can I lodge my BAS from it?

It gives you totals to check against your BAS. You still lodge through ATO online services or your agent.

I'm not GST-registered. Is it still useful?

Yes. Set GST to No in the tracker or the kit, and the totals still work.

Do the planner's BAS dates apply to me?

Only if you're GST-registered and report quarterly. Lodging online or through an agent may give you more time. Check ato.gov.au.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Insert an image at the top of each sheet or document.
