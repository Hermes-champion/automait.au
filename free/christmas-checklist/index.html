Christmas trading-hours checklist for Australian small businesses

Closing or cutting hours over Christmas 2026 and New Year 2027? This one-page A4 checklist covers what to update, who to tell and when, with the 2026–27 public holiday dates on the page.

Download the PDF

1 page, A4, about 23 KB. Free to print and use for your own business.

What's on the checklist

Grouped by when to do it: 3–4 weeks before, 1–2 weeks before, your last day, and back in January. Every item has a tick box.

  • Your dates: last trading day, reopening day, last quotes and orders, final invoices, urgent contact
  • Door or window sign: printed and up 1–2 weeks before you close
  • Google Business Profile special hours: for every closed or changed day
  • Website and socials banner: your closed and reopening dates, pinned
  • Email auto-reply and voicemail message: with short example wording you can copy
  • Last invoice and quote dates: plus chasing anything overdue
  • Supplier cut-offs: final orders and their reopening dates
  • Staff leave and rosters: with a pointer to Fair Work for public holiday pay
  • Reopening in January: auto-reply off, normal hours back, a short 'we're open again' post
  • Public holiday dates 2026–27: Christmas to Australia Day, by state, from Fair Work
