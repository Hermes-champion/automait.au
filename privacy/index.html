Privacy Policy

AUTOMAIT (ABN 82 801 716 831) ("we", "us") · Last updated 10 Nov 2026

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

1. What we collect

  • When you email us: your name, email address, business details and anything else you choose to include in your message.
  • When you buy a digital product (such as the AI Front Desk Kit, the AI Operator Pack or our business templates) or pay for a service: your name, email address and billing details. Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number. Product files are delivered as a direct download hosted on Google Drive; no Google sign-in is needed, and we don't receive your details from Google.
  • When you buy one of our products on Gumroad: Gumroad (a US company) takes your payment and handles the sale under its own privacy policy. Gumroad shares your email address, any name you give, your country and your purchase details with us so we can support you and process refunds. We don't receive your card details.
  • When you buy on Etsy: If you buy one of our templates on Etsy, Etsy collects your details and shares order information with us under Etsy's privacy policy; we use it only to fulfil and support your order.
  • When we set up a service for your business: we may need access to your business accounts (for example your email, calendar, website form or Google Business Profile) and to information about your customers that passes through them (such as names, phone numbers, emails and job details). We handle this only to provide the service you asked for.
  • Website: our site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may log technical data such as IP addresses. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking analytics. The demo assistant on our homepage runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type into it is sent to us or stored. The free calculators at /tools/ also run entirely in your browser: nothing you type into them is sent to us or stored. If you choose to use it to request a quote, it opens an email in your own email app, which reaches us only if you press send.

2. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries, quote, deliver what you bought, and provide support and refunds.
  • To keep records for tax and legal purposes.
  • To build, test and maintain services you engage us for.

We do not send unsolicited marketing. We only email you about your enquiry, purchase or service. If we ever send newsletters, it will only be with your consent, and every message will include an unsubscribe option.

3. AI processing

AUTOMAIT is run by its founder working with an AI operator. Our AI agent reads and replies to emails and builds services. This means your messages, and any information you give us, are processed by AI service providers. A person from AUTOMAIT is accountable for the business and can review any request. Just ask.

4. Who we share it with

We only share information with service providers who help us run the business, such as:

  • Stripe (payments, including buy-now-pay-later providers such as Klarna if you choose them)
  • Gumroad, Inc. (USA), for products bought on Gumroad
  • Our email provider
  • AI model providers (e.g. Anthropic)
  • GitHub (website hosting) and Google Fonts (web fonts)
  • Google (file delivery via Google Drive)

Some of these providers store or process data outside Australia (for example in the United States). We choose reputable providers and share only what is needed.

We do not sell personal information. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

5. Your customers' information

When we build a service for your business, you remain responsible for your own customers' personal information. We act on your instructions, use it only to provide the service, do not use it for any other purpose, and will delete or return it when the engagement ends, on request.

6. Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. We keep it only as long as we need it, including for tax records (generally 5 years), and then delete it.

7. Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or make a privacy complaint, by emailing hello@automait.au. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version will always be on this page.
