Christmas closure notice 2026: what dates to include, where to post it, and how to tell staff

By AUTOMAIT · 11 November 2026

Christmas Day 2026 is a Friday and Boxing Day is a Saturday, so every state and territory gets an extra public holiday on Monday 28 December. New Year's Day is Friday 1 January 2027. If you're closing over the break, your notice needs three things: the last day you're open, the day you reopen, and how people can reach you if something can't wait. If you want staff to take annual leave while you're shut, sort that out first, because some awards need four weeks' written notice.

Dates checked against the Fair Work Ombudsman's 2026 and 2027 public holiday pages on 11 November 2026. Fair Work says it includes "state- or territory-wide holidays, capital city holidays and some regional holidays", so check your state's own list for local days.

General information only, not legal or workplace advice. For your situation, check your award or agreement, or call the Fair Work Infoline on 13 13 94.

Quick answer: the 2026–27 holidays to plan around are Friday 25 December (Christmas Day, everywhere), Saturday 26 December (Boxing Day everywhere except Tasmania; called the Proclamation Day holiday in SA), Monday 28 December (a public holiday everywhere) and Friday 1 January 2027 (New Year's Day, everywhere). Put your closure dates on your door, your Google Business Profile as special hours, your website, your email auto-reply and voicemail, and your last invoices for the year. To direct staff to take annual leave during the closure, your award or agreement has to allow it. The General Retail, Clerks and Hospitality awards ask for 28 days' written notice, and for a shutdown starting Thursday 24 December that means written notice by Thursday 26 November at the latest.

The dates to put on your notice

List your last day open, first day back, last day for orders and an urgent contact. Then check which public holidays fall inside your break.

Sunday 27 December, and Tuesday 29 to Thursday 31 December, aren't on any of Fair Work's state lists as public holidays (apart from the New Year's Eve evening in the NT and SA). If you reopen between Christmas and New Year, those are ordinary days for pay purposes, though your award may still have weekend or evening rates.

Write the weekday next to every date. It's much harder to misread than 23/12.

Where to post it

Put the same dates everywhere people might look:

  • A sign on the door or window, up a week or two before you close.
  • Your Google Business Profile, using special hours (more below).
  • A banner on your website and a pinned post on your socials.
  • Your email auto-reply and voicemail greeting, with the reopening date.
  • Your last quotes and invoices for the year, and an email to suppliers and regular clients. Keep it to the facts; if you add an offer, the Spam Act's consent and unsubscribe rules apply.

Google Business Profile special hours

Google's help page says: "You can set special hours if your business temporarily adjusts its hours of operation or remains closed for up to 6 days in a row." In your Business Profile, go to Edit profile, then Hours, then Edit next to "Special hours", and tick "Closed" for each day you're shut (Google Business Profile Help).

The same page says "For special hours to show up on your Business Profile, set your business hours to “Open with main hours.”" Google also says: "If your business is closed for 7 or more days in a row, or will be closed for an unknown period of time, you should mark your business as Temporarily closed." A break from Christmas Eve into January is usually 7 days or more, so read that part of Google's help page before you choose between special hours and "Temporarily closed".

Telling staff, and the annual leave question

Fair Work calls a Christmas closure a shutdown: "A shutdown is when a business temporarily closes, such as during the Christmas and New Year period." Can you make staff take annual leave while you're shut? Fair Work says "An employee can be directed to take annual leave during a shutdown if their award or enterprise agreement allows it." (Fair Work)

For staff who aren't covered by an award or enterprise agreement, Fair Work says "An employer can require employees who aren't covered by an award or enterprise agreement to take paid annual leave if the requirement is reasonable."

What the awards say

We read the shutdown clause in three awards: the General Retail Industry Award 2020 (clause 28.4), the Clerks—Private Sector Award 2020 (clause 32.5) and the Hospitality Industry (General) Award 2020 (clause 30.4). All three say the same things:

  • "The employer must give the affected employees 28 days’ written notice of a temporary shutdown period, or any shorter period agreed between the employer and the majority of relevant employees."
  • A direction to take annual leave "must be in writing" and "must be reasonable".
  • You can only direct someone to take paid annual leave they've already accrued.
  • Staff hired after you give notice must get written notice "as soon as reasonably practicable" after they start.
  • For days not covered by a direction, you can agree in writing on leave without pay, or on annual leave in advance under the award.

Other awards and enterprise agreements can say something different, so look yours up with Fair Work's Find my award tool.

On timing: if your shutdown starts on Thursday 24 December 2026, give written notice by Thursday 26 November at the latest. Earlier is safer. If you close earlier, count back 28 days from your first closed day.

Public holidays inside the shutdown

Public holidays that fall inside your shutdown aren't annual leave days. That means Christmas Day, Monday 28 December and New Year's Day everywhere, Saturday 26 December everywhere except Tasmania, and the Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve evening holidays in Queensland, the NT and SA. Fair Work says that if a public holiday falls during a period of paid leave, "the employee has to be paid for the public holiday", including "any hours that fall on a part-day public holiday", and "The public holiday isn't counted as annual leave or sick leave under the NES." (Fair Work).

A shutdown also isn't a stand down, which Fair Work describes as staff not being usefully employed "for reasons outside the employer’s control".

An example notice you can copy

For customers. Swap the bracketed parts for your details.

Closed for Christmas and New Year

[Business name] will close at [3 pm] on [Wednesday 23 December 2026] and reopen at [8 am] on [Monday 4 January 2027].

Last day for orders and bookings: [Friday 18 December]. Anything that comes in after that will be handled from [Monday 4 January].

Need us urgently? [Call or text 04xx xxx xxx / email you@yourbusiness.com.au] and we'll reply [within one business day / when we can].

Thanks for your business this year. See you in 2027.

For staff, put it in writing and date it. Include the shutdown dates, the public holidays inside it, how many days of leave each person needs, what happens if someone doesn't have enough (check your award), and who to ask.

Frequently asked questions

When are the Christmas and New Year public holidays in 2026–27?

Everywhere: Friday 25 December 2026, Monday 28 December 2026 and Friday 1 January 2027. Saturday 26 December is also a holiday everywhere except Tasmania. Queensland, the NT and SA have an evening holiday on Christmas Eve, and the NT and SA on New Year's Eve.

Is Monday 28 December 2026 a public holiday?

Yes, in every state and territory. In Tasmania it's Boxing Day itself; elsewhere it's the additional holiday for Boxing Day (Proclamation Day in SA).

How much notice do I have to give staff for a Christmas shutdown?

It depends on your award or agreement. The General Retail, Clerks and Hospitality awards each require 28 days' written notice, or less if the majority of affected employees agree. Other awards can differ, so check yours.

Can I make staff take annual leave while we're closed over Christmas?

Only if their award or enterprise agreement allows it. For employees not covered by an award or agreement, Fair Work says you can require paid annual leave if the requirement is reasonable.

How do I show Christmas closure hours on Google?

For a closure of up to 6 days in a row, set special hours on your Google Business Profile and tick "Closed" for each day. Google says a business closed for 7 or more days in a row should be marked as Temporarily closed instead, so check Google's help page if your break is longer.
