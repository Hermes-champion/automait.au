2027 public holidays in Australia by state: a planning list for small businesses

By AUTOMAIT · 11 November 2026

Christmas Day 2027 is a Saturday and Boxing Day is a Sunday, so every state and territory also gets public holidays on Monday 27 and Tuesday 28 December. If you're writing December rosters or a closure notice, start there. The rest of the year is below, state by state.

Dates checked against the Fair Work Ombudsman's 2027 public holidays page on 11 November 2026 (the page was last updated 28 October 2026). Fair Work says its list is "only a guide" and doesn't cover every regional holiday, so check your state or territory's own list as well. Each state section below links to it (Fair Work).

General information only, not legal, tax or workplace advice. For your situation, check your award or agreement, call the Fair Work Infoline on 13 13 94, or talk to a registered tax or BAS agent.

Quick answer: every state and territory has New Year's Day (Fri 1 January), Australia Day (Tue 26 January), Good Friday (Fri 26 March), Easter Monday (Mon 29 March), Christmas Day (Sat 25 December) and holidays on Mon 27 and Tue 28 December. Anzac Day is Sunday 25 April, with a Monday holiday on 26 April in the ACT, NSW, NT, Queensland and WA. King's Birthday is Mon 14 June everywhere except WA (27 September) and Queensland (4 October). Labour Day moves around the most: 1 March in WA, 8 March in Victoria (Eight Hours Day in Tasmania), 3 May in Queensland (May Day in the NT) and 4 October in the ACT, NSW and SA. Victoria's AFL Grand Final eve holiday has no date yet.

What this means for a small business

Rosters and pay

Employees have a right to be away from work on a public holiday. You can ask someone to work, but the request has to be reasonable, and they can refuse if they have reasonable grounds or the request isn't reasonable. If they take the day off, employees other than casuals who'd normally work that day are paid their base rate for the ordinary hours they would have worked (Fair Work).

On top of that, your award or agreement can set extra pay for working a public holiday, minimum shift lengths, and whether you can agree to swap a public holiday for another day (Fair Work). Look yours up with Fair Work's Find my award tool before you set December and Easter rosters.

Closures and long weekends

Two stretches stand out. Easter runs Friday 26 to Monday 29 March, and in most states Saturday and Sunday are public holidays too. At the end of the year, Saturday 25 to Tuesday 28 December are holidays in most states, so if you close over Christmas, tell customers and suppliers early. If you trade late, remember the evening holidays: Christmas Eve from 6 pm in Queensland, and Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve from 7 pm in the NT and SA.

BAS due dates

"If the due date is on a weekend or public holiday, you have until the next business day to lodge and pay" (ATO). The ATO counts a public holiday that applies to the whole of any state or territory, and then taxpayers in every state can use the next business day (ATO). So the December quarter BAS, due 28 February 2027 (a Sunday), moves past WA's Labour Day on Monday 1 March, and the ATO's table shows Tuesday 2 March as the first business day. The full list of 2026–27 dates is in our post on BAS due dates 2026–27.

Frequently asked questions

When are the Christmas public holidays in 2027?

Christmas Day is Saturday 25 December 2027. Every state and territory also has public holidays on Monday 27 December (an additional day for Christmas Day) and Tuesday 28 December (Boxing Day in Tasmania, and an additional Boxing Day or Proclamation Day holiday everywhere else). Sunday 26 December is Boxing Day in most states, and the Proclamation Day holiday in SA.

Is there a Monday public holiday for Anzac Day 2027?

Anzac Day falls on Sunday 25 April 2027. The ACT, NSW, NT, Queensland and WA have a public holiday on Monday 26 April. Fair Work's lists for South Australia, Tasmania and Victoria show the Sunday only.

When is the King's Birthday public holiday in 2027?

Monday 14 June 2027 in the ACT, NSW, NT, SA, Tasmania and Victoria. Western Australia's is Monday 27 September (some regional areas differ) and Queensland's is Monday 4 October.

When is the AFL Grand Final public holiday in 2027?

It hasn't been set yet. Fair Work lists Victoria's Friday before the AFL Grand Final as "Subject to AFL schedule (date TBC)". Check Business Victoria once the AFL fixture is out.

Do public holidays change BAS due dates?

They can. The ATO says if a due date is on a weekend or public holiday, you have until the next business day to lodge and pay. It counts a holiday that applies to the whole of any state or territory, for taxpayers in every state. For example, 28 February 2027 is a Sunday and Monday 1 March is Labour Day in WA, so the ATO's table shows Tuesday 2 March as the first business day. Check the due date on your BAS.
