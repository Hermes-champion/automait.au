EOFY 2027 checklist for Australian sole traders

By AUTOMAIT · 13 November 2026

The 2026–27 financial year ends on Wednesday 30 June 2027. Anything you want counted in this year (a tool you start using, a super contribution reaching your fund) has to happen by then. After it come three dates: the June quarter BAS on 28 July 2027, the TPAR on 28 August 2027 if it applies to you, and your tax return by 31 October 2027 if you lodge it yourself.

Dates and rules checked on the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) website on 12 November 2026. The ATO can change them before June, so check again in May 2027.

General information only, not tax or financial advice. For your situation, talk to a registered tax or BAS agent or contact the ATO.

Quick answer: before Wed 30 June 2027, have any tool or equipment you'll claim this year in use or installed (eligible items costing under $20,000 each may be written off straight away, for the business portion, if your aggregated turnover is under $10 million), and get any personal super contribution to your fund, with a notice of intent if you'll claim it. Then lodge: STP finalisation by 14 July 2027 if you have employees, Q4 BAS by 28 July 2027, TPAR by 28 August 2027 if you pay contractors in building and construction, cleaning, courier and road freight, IT, or security, investigation or surveillance, and your tax return by 31 October 2027, or later through a tax agent.

Before 30 June 2027

1. Tools and equipment: the $20,000 instant asset write-off

From 1 July 2026 the $20,000 instant asset write-off is permanent. If your aggregated turnover is under $10 million, you may be able to claim an immediate deduction for the business portion of an eligible asset that costs less than $20,000 and is first used, or installed ready for use, in that income year (ATO).

  • Per asset: the limit applies to each asset, so several items can each qualify if each costs under $20,000 (ATO).
  • Timing: you claim it in the year the asset is first used or installed ready for use (ATO). For 2026–27, that means in use or installed ready for use by 30 June 2027. Paying for something that hasn't arrived isn't enough.
  • Business portion only: if a ute or a laptop is partly private, you claim only the business share (ATO).
  • Dearer items: assets costing $20,000 or more can go into the small business pool, depreciated at 15% in the first year and 30% after that. A pool balance under $20,000 at the end of the year can be written off (ATO).
  • Opted out before? If you previously chose not to use the simplified depreciation rules, you can re-enter until 30 June 2027 without waiting out the usual 5-year lock-out (ATO).

A write-off lowers your taxable income; it isn't money back. Buy what the business needs, not something to chase a deduction.

2. Super for yourself

As a sole trader nobody pays super for you. If you put money in yourself and want to claim it as a tax deduction:

  • Get it to the fund by 30 June 2027. Contributions count towards a cap in the year your super fund receives them (ATO).
  • Know the cap. The concessional contributions cap is $32,500 from 1 July 2026, and personal contributions you claim as a deduction count towards it (ATO).
  • Send a notice of intent. You must give your fund a notice of intent in the approved form and get its acknowledgment before you claim the deduction on your tax return (ATO).

3. If you have employees: payday super

Payday super started on 1 July 2026, so 2026–27 is its first year. Super guarantee contributions must be received by your employees' super funds generally within 7 business days after you pay them, calculated as 12% of qualifying earnings and reported through Single Touch Payroll. The Small Business Superannuation Clearing House has closed (ATO). If you still pay super quarterly, talk to your agent or the ATO now, not in June. Make your Single Touch Payroll finalisation declaration by 14 July 2027 so your employees' income statements are tax ready (later dates can apply for some closely held payees or with an ATO deferral) (ATO).

4. Records

You need to keep most business records for 5 years. They must be in English (or easy to convert) and stored so they can't be changed or damaged (ATO). Before 30 June, match your receipts and invoices to your bank account and chase any missing ones while people still remember the job.

After 30 June 2027

  • June quarter BAS: due Wed 28 July 2027, or 25 August 2027 through a registered agent for eligible clients who lodge electronically (ATO, ATO).
  • TPAR: a TPAR must be lodged by 28 August each year. It applies to businesses providing building and construction, cleaning, courier and road freight, information technology (IT), or security, investigation or surveillance services that pay contractors to deliver them (ATO). 28 August 2027 is a Saturday, so you can lodge on Monday 30 August 2027, the next business day (ATO).
  • Tax return: most registered tax agents can lodge after the usual 31 October deadline, but you should contact them before 31 October to be part of their lodgment program (ATO). Lodging yourself, 31 October 2027 is a Sunday, so you have until Monday 1 November 2027 (ATO).

Frequently asked questions

When is EOFY 2027 in Australia?

The 2026–27 financial year ends on Wednesday 30 June 2027. The 2027–28 year starts on Thursday 1 July 2027.

When is my 2027 tax return due as a sole trader?

If you lodge it yourself, the usual deadline is 31 October. 31 October 2027 is a Sunday, and the ATO lets you lodge on the next business day when a due date falls on a weekend. Most registered tax agents can lodge later under their lodgment program, but you need to contact them before 31 October to be part of it.

Is the $20,000 instant asset write-off still available for 2026–27?

Yes. The ATO says that from 1 July 2026 the $20,000 instant asset write-off is permanent for businesses with aggregated turnover under $10 million. The limit applies to each asset, and you claim it in the year the asset is first used or installed ready for use.

What's the deadline for super contributions to count in 2026–27?

Contributions count towards a cap in the year your super fund receives them, so a contribution has to reach your fund by 30 June 2027 to count in 2026–27. Payments can take days to clear, so don't leave it to the last day. The concessional cap is $32,500 from 1 July 2026.

When is the June quarter 2027 BAS due?

The April to June 2027 quarterly BAS is due Wednesday 28 July 2027. Registered agents lodging electronically for eligible clients have until 25 August 2027.
